## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: `app-store` / `polished`
- Creative direction: Sleek GovTech launch for a 24/7 Nagpur Seva Kendra
- Interpretation: Crisp civic typography, authentic bilingual Marathi/English badges, high-contrast palette (Heritage Pine + Emerald + Saffron Gold), rapid but easily readable cards with generous hold times.
- Angle: Modernize the traditional Indian cyber cafe/Seva Kendra into an ultra-clean, zero-queue digital service with automated privacy deletion and instant tracking.
- Hook: "Tired of standing in Seva Kendra queues at 9 AM?"
- Outro / punchline: "सर्व शासकीय आणि खाजगी सेवा एका छताखाली · Open 24 Hours · Ayodhya Nagar, Nagpur"
- Avoid:
  * Generic SaaS jargon ("synergistic workflows")
  * Abstract 3D filler shapes
  * Disconnected color palettes
