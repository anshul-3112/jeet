# Brag Plan: Jeet Digital E-Governance Seva Kendra

## What is this app?
Jeet Digital E-Governance Seva Kendra is a production-grade, 24/7 digital portal for an authorized Aaple Sarkar Seva Kendra in Nagpur (Ayodhya Nagar Square) that lets citizens upload government application documents without logging in, get instant tracking codes, and guarantees a 24-hour privacy auto-purge.

## The angle
Neighborhood cyber cafes in India are notorious for physical paper queues, misplaced documents, and uncertain turnarounds. Jeet Digital flips this completely: a neighborhood Seva Kendra run with the precision and sleekness of modern GovTech SaaS — direct mobile camera upload, live WhatsApp status pings, Razorpay payments, and an automated 24-hour file purge.
